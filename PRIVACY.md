# Privacy And Child Safety Draft

IQ Lab is cognitive entertainment and practice. It is not a clinical IQ test, medical device, diagnostic tool or treatment.

## Data Collected

The current app stores progress locally on the device: XP, streak, sessions, favourites, best scores, difficulty mode and compact session metrics. No account, name, age, date of birth, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, biometric data or advertising ID is collected.

## Network

The current app does not require a backend or online service for gameplay.

## Export And Import

Backups are user-controlled and versioned. They include local progress data only. Users should store exported files carefully because anyone with the file may inspect progress values.

## Children

The app avoids ads, public leaderboards, social profiles and manipulative pressure. Competition features are local and optional.

This document is an implementation-support draft and not a substitute for qualified legal review.
