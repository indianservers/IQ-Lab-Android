## Title

IQ Lab: Brain Games

## Short Description

## Full Description

IQ Lab is a local-first collection of 150 brain games for practising memory, mathematics, logic, reading, attention, processing speed and spatial reasoning.

Train through five levels, from friendly Explorer activities to demanding Genius challenges. Track XP, personal bests, mastery stars, achievements and local progress trends. Daily Arena, Weekly Cup and share-code challenges work without an account or backend.

IQ Lab is for cognitive entertainment and practice. It is not a medical product, clinical assessment or official IQ test.

## Feature Bullets

- 150 playable brain-game activities.
- Five levels: Explorer, Challenger, Thinker, Mastermind and Genius.
- Adaptive, Fixed and Relaxed difficulty modes.
- Local progress, mastery stars and achievements.
- Daily and weekly local challenge modes.
- No account required.
- Offline-first gameplay.
